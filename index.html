FAQ

Questions, answered

What is Wapora?

Wapora is a WhatsApp CRM and marketing platform. It gives you campaigns, a shared team inbox, automations and a lead pipeline, all connected to your own WhatsApp Business number.

Do I need the WhatsApp Business API?

Yes. Wapora runs on the official WhatsApp Cloud API from Meta. You connect your own API credentials and we handle the rest, with a guide inside the dashboard that walks you through it.

Do you charge me per message?

No. Conversation charges go straight to Meta at Meta's rates. Wapora charges a flat software fee and nothing on top of your messages.

Can my whole team use one number?

Yes. The shared inbox is built for teams. Everyone works from the same conversations, and you can see who handled what.

Can I send bulk campaigns?

Yes. You can broadcast an approved template to all your opted-in contacts at once, with each person's details filled in automatically.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Your tokens are encrypted, every business is fully isolated from every other, and you can export your data whenever you want.

Whose number is it, mine or yours?

Yours. It is registered under your own Meta WhatsApp Business account. If you ever leave Wapora, your number and your data go with you.

How long does setup take?

Most of it is connecting your Meta credentials, which takes a few minutes with the in-dashboard guide. After that you set up templates and your team, and you are ready to send.
